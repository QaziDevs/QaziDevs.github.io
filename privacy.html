Crumble Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

QaziDevs ("we") made the game Crumble Hop: Jump Adventure. This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, lifelines, characters and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. The game has no accounts or passwords.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. In some regions you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing and friend challenges

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture and a message on your device and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with. A friend challenge contains the level, your time, the moves of your run and the name you typed; it is only shared when you send it yourself.

Online play

If you use online play (races, rooms or the Knockout Cup), the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase. It stores the name you choose, your character, your moves and times in races, and your tournament times, linked to a random id. Other players in the same race, room or tournament group see your name, character and times. We do not collect your email or other personal details for this. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Online data is kept only as long as it is needed: race data is deleted when the players leave a race, and your tournament entries about a week after the tournament.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game reminds you about your daily reward, today's event and free spin, the Knockout Cup (when it starts and when your next round begins) and new daily goals. These reminders are scheduled on your phone itself; nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings (Reminders) or in your phone's notification settings.

Purchases

The game currently has no in-app purchases. If we add them in a future update, this policy will be updated first.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything saved on your device. To have your online data (name, character, race and tournament times) deleted sooner, email us at likealife17@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
